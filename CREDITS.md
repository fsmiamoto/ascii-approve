# Credits

ASCII art is folk art: most pieces below have circulated on Usenet and ASCII archives for decades. Artist signatures are kept inside the art wherever the original had them. If you are an artist listed here and want a piece removed or credited differently, open an issue.

Text banners are rendered with [pyfiglet](https://github.com/pwaller/pyfiglet) fonts.

- **Sanic approves** (`sanic`) — Flavio Miamoto
- **Ship it** (`shipit`) — Ship: unknown artist (asciiart.eu); text: figlet ansi_shadow
- **LGTM** (`lgtm`) — Thumbs up: Donovan Baker (dwb); text: figlet ansi_shadow
- **F1 — Lights out** (`f1-lights-out`) — Car: jrei (ascii.co.uk); text: figlet slant
- **F1 — Front row** (`f1-front-row`) — Seal do Mar (rec.arts.ascii, 1996)
- **F1 — Ship-it wing** (`f1-ship-it-wing`) — Adapted from jrei (ascii.co.uk) — rear-wing text changed
- **Gotta go fast** (`gotta-go-fast`) — Sonic: Han J. Lee; text: figlet slant
- **Speedrun** (`speedrun`) — Runner: Joan G. Stark (jgs)
- **Merge award** (`merge-award`) — Adapted from Joan G. Stark (jgs) — "ASCII AWARD" → "MERGE AWARD"
- **Fireworks** (`fireworks`) — Joan G. Stark (jgs)
- **Rawr** (`rawr`) — Happy Dinosaur: unknown artist (asciiart.eu)
- **Beep boop** (`beep-boop`) — Robot: Michael J. Penick (MJP)
- **Espadon approves** (`espadon`) — unknown

Sources: [asciiart.eu](https://www.asciiart.eu), [ascii.co.uk](https://ascii.co.uk), rec.arts.ascii / alt.ascii-art archives.
